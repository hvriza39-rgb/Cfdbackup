04 Before you join

The questions we get most.

If yours isn't here, just ask — we'd rather answer it upfront than have you guess.

Do I need any experience to join?

None at all. AWR is built for every level — from complete beginners who've never placed a trade to experienced investors and retirees looking to sharpen their strategy. You choose your experience tier when you join, and the community meets you where you are.

What does membership cost?

Membership is priced in tiers based on the level of guidance you want — from self-paced access to live sessions with senior members. Reach out on any of the channels above and we'll send you the current pricing and what's included.

Is this financial advice?

No. AWR is an education and community platform. We provide market insights, educational resources, and trading guidance — but every decision you make is your own. We're here to help you understand the landscape, not to tell you what to buy.

How much time does it take?

As much or as little as you want. Some members check in daily, follow the market briefs, and join every live session. Others dip in once a week for the longer-form content. The material is structured so you can move at your own pace without falling behind.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no lock-ins and no long contracts. Cancel whenever you like from your account — you'll keep access until the end of your billing period, and you're welcome back any time.

If you're ever unsure whether it's the right fit, message us. We'd rather have an honest conversation than keep someone paying for something that isn't working for them.
