House rules

How we keep the room sharp.

A community is only as good as its standards. These six are enforced by the desk, kindly but firmly.

01

No get-rich-quick

Promotions, pumps and “guaranteed returns” get you removed. Compounding is the only magic allowed here.

02

Give before you ask

Answer a question today, ask one tomorrow. The network pays back those who pay in.

03

Sources over vibes

Share the data or the article behind your take. Strong opinions, held loosely.

04

Privacy stays inside

What members share about their numbers stays in the room. Full stop.

05

Beginners welcome

No jargon-gatekeeping. Explain it so a smart sixteen-year-old could follow.

06

Disagree well

Attack the argument, never the person. The best idea usually comes from the other side.
