Chapter II

Four phases, no mystery.

Every member's journey follows the same spine. It isn't glamorous, but it's predictable — and predictability is exactly what lets you plan around it.

  1. i

    Discover

    An honest intake: where you are, what you want, and how much risk you can genuinely stomach. The conversation comes before the spreadsheet.

  2. ii

    Design

    A plan tailored to your timeline, not a template. Allocation, contributions, income targets and review points — pressure-tested against your own numbers.

  3. iii

    Deploy

    Execute with the community at your side. Weekly rhythm, transparent reporting, named points of contact. If something isn't working, you'll hear it first.

  4. iv

    Compound

    Stay the course. Reviews, rebalancing, and the discipline to let time do its work — plus the legacy planning that keeps it going after you.

Chapter IV

The questions we get most.

If yours isn't here, just ask — we'd rather answer it upfront than have you guess.

How do I become a member?

Reach out on Telegram or WhatsApp with two or three sentences about where you are financially and what you're hoping to build. If the Club is a good fit, we'll send you an invite to the community and a short intake form. Most members are in within a week.

Do I need to be wealthy to join?

No. We have members at every stage — from their first £500 index fund to seven-figure portfolios they're positioning for retirement. The problems change in scale, but the principles don't, and the conversation is far richer for the mix.

What does membership cost?

We run a small number of tiers depending on how much access and support you want — from a self-serve community tier to a fully managed one with regular portfolio reviews. We quote a flat monthly figure, never a percentage of assets, so incentives stay aligned.

Is my information confidential?

Completely. Anything you share in a portfolio review or a one-to-one stays between you and the practitioner you're speaking with. The community itself operates on a simple rule: what's said inside, stays inside.

Can I leave anytime?

Yes. Memberships are month-to-month after the first quarter, and you're free to cancel at any point — no exit interview, no retention call. We'd rather people come back when the timing is right than feel stuck inside something that isn't working.
