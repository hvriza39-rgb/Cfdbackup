02 How we work

Four phases, no mystery.

Every engagement follows the same spine. It's not glamorous, but it's predictable — and predictability is what lets you plan around us.

Phase 01

Diagnose

Two to three weeks of interviews, data pulls and site visits. We come back with a short, blunt read on what's actually happening — including the parts nobody wanted to say out loud.

Phase 02

Design

Options, not answers. We build two or three credible paths with real numbers attached, so your leadership team can choose with eyes open rather than being sold a single recommendation.

Phase 03

Deliver

We work inside your teams, not alongside them. Weekly delivery rhythm, named owners, visible metrics. If something isn't working, you'll hear it from us first.

Phase 04

Embed

The goal is to become unnecessary. We hand over the models, the playbooks and the routines, then stay on call for a quarter at no extra cost while your team takes the wheel.

04 Before you write

The questions we get most.

If yours isn't here, just ask — we'd rather answer it upfront than have you guess.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within two to three weeks of a signed scope. If it's genuinely urgent — a deal closing, a board deadline, a system failing — we can often mobilise a small team in under a week. Ask, and we'll be honest about what's possible.

What does a typical engagement cost?

Diagnostic sprints start in the low five figures. Full transformation programmes run into six figures, sometimes more. We quote a fixed fee against a fixed scope, so you know the number before anything begins — and we'll tell you plainly if we think you don't need us.

Do you work with smaller companies?

Yes. Roughly a third of our work is with companies between 20 and 300 people. The problems are different in shape but not in kind, and the pace is often faster because decisions take hours rather than quarters.

Who actually does the work?

The people you meet in the first conversation. We don't run a pyramid model with partners selling and juniors delivering — every engagement is staffed with at least two senior practitioners who stay from kick-off to close-out.

Can you work alongside our existing advisors?

Often, yes — particularly on transactions where lawyers, accountants and technical specialists are already involved. We're comfortable being one workstream among several, and we're equally comfortable coordinating the whole thing if that's what you need.

What we won't do is duplicate someone else's scope. If another firm is already covering the ground, we'll say so and suggest a narrower role.
